02 / A little about me

I study the forces that make people, places, and institutions change.

I’m a history student who learns about the past to better understand the world around us. I enjoy tracing the people, movements, and turning points that shaped societies—and asking how those stories still influence the choices we make today.

Through research, reading, and writing, I’m building the habit of looking closely at evidence, considering different perspectives, and communicating ideas with clarity. I’m especially interested in the connections between history, culture, institutions, and the future of work.

This portfolio is where I’ll share research projects, essays, and new skills as I develop them. I’m eager to bring curiosity, thoughtful analysis, and a strong work ethic to an internship or early-career opportunity.
